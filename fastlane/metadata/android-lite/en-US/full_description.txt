Pauca Lite turns your phone into something closer to a dumbphone: your apps become words in calm cards, with the names you choose. No icons, no red badges, no ads, no tracking.

Your home screen
• Apps as plain text in cards: rename, reorder and group them
• Swipe up for a drawer with every app, plus search and categories
• Swipe down for notifications, sideways to open the apps you pick
• Clock, date and light or dark themes
• Available in 25 languages
• Two profiles, each with its own cards
• Light or dark theme, a few accent colors, and text and clock size

Private
No ads, no tracking, no data collected. Pauca Lite has no internet permission and no accessibility service.

Want more? Get Pauca
Pauca Lite is the free version. Pauca, the full version, is a one-time purchase with no subscription. It adds focus mode, unlimited profiles, pause to breathe, weather on the home screen, double tap to lock, and more themes, colors and fonts. When you install Pauca, your cards and settings from Lite come along.

Open source (GPLv3), based on Olauncher by Tanuj.
